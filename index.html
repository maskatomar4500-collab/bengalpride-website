Our Story

Crafted from the
Heart of Bengal

Bengal Pride was born from a love for the ancient craft traditions of Bangladesh — the skilled hands that weave cane, hogla, and shitol paty into objects of everyday beauty. Each product you receive carries the warmth of a local artisan's craft and the spirit of a greener, more conscious world.

We believe sustainability isn't a trend — it's a return to how things were always meant to be made: from nature, with care, and designed to last.
